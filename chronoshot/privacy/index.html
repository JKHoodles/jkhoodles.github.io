Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

ChronoShot collects no data. Your videos, sequences and exports stay on your iPhone. All analysis, subject detection and rendering happen on the device. ChronoShot has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never see, receive or sell anything about you.

Who we are

ChronoShot is made by JKHoodles Pty Ltd, Australia ("we"). This policy explains what ChronoShot does with information on your device. If anything here is unclear, contact us at andy.kim.jkhoodles@gmail.com.

What ChronoShot does not collect

ChronoShot does not collect, store on any server, transmit or share personal data. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. That is why its App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

Your photo library

  • Choosing videos and photos. ChronoShot uses Apple's photo picker, which runs outside the app. ChronoShot receives only the video or photo you pick, and cannot see the rest of your library. If the item is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may download it to your iPhone; that happens between your device and Apple.
  • Saving. When you export, ChronoShot asks for permission to add the finished image, GIF or video to your library. It only adds; it does not read anything back.

You can change the saving permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Camera and people in your videos

ChronoShot does not use the camera. To cut out the athlete, it finds people and objects in the frames of the video you chose, using Apple's on-device Vision framework. This is not face recognition and does not identify anyone; the results are only used to build your picture and stay on your iPhone.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your sequences: a copy of each video you import, the cut-outs, backgrounds and edits made from it, and thumbnails.
  • For each imported video, Apple's identifier for the item it came from in Photos, so ChronoShot can ask you to find the same video again if its copy is ever missing.
  • A count of finished free exports, kept in the iPhone's keychain so the free allowance is not reset by reinstalling.

None of this leaves your iPhone through ChronoShot. Like any app's data, it may be included in backups of your iPhone that you make to iCloud or a computer, under Apple's terms. Deleting a sequence deletes its copy of the video and its edits; your original in Photos is not affected. Deleting ChronoShot deletes all your sequences.

Purchases

ChronoShot Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages subscriptions; we never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account. ChronoShot only asks Apple, through StoreKit on your device, whether Pro is active. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

When you tap Share, iOS shows its share sheet and the file goes only where you send it.

Children

ChronoShot collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything ChronoShot keeps is on your iPhone and under your control.

Changes

If ChronoShot ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
