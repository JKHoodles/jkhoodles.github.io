How do I get my videos into CineManiac?

Any of these: copy them in with the Finder on your Mac (select your iPhone, then Files → CineManiac); send them with AirDrop or the Share menu; or, in CineManiac's Files tab, tap Add Folder… to play straight from iCloud Drive, a USB drive or another app without copying.

Which formats does it play?

Containers: MKV, MP4, MOV, AVI (Xvid, DivX), WMV, ASF, FLV, MPEG, VOB, TS, M2TS, WebM and 3GP. Video: H.264 and HEVC (including 4K and 10-bit) with hardware decoding, plus MPEG-4, DivX, MPEG-2, WMV, VP8, ProRes and MJPEG. Audio: AAC, MP3, AC-3, E-AC-3, FLAC, ALAC, Opus, Vorbis, WMA and PCM, up to 7.1.

A video plays without sound.

Its audio is probably DTS or Dolby TrueHD, which CineManiac can't decode. If the file has another audio track, choose it in the player's Audio & Subtitles panel (the speech-bubble button).

How do I show subtitles?

Put a subtitle file (SRT, ASS, WebVTT, SMI or MicroDVD) in the same folder as the video with the same name, for example Film.mkv and Film.srt. CineManiac finds it automatically. You can also add one from the player's Audio & Subtitles panel. If the text looks garbled, choose its language under Text Encoding in the same panel.

The subtitles are early or late.

In Audio & Subtitles, use Subtitle Timing to move them in tenth-of-a-second steps. If they start right but drift further out as the film goes on, choose a Speed that matches the frame rates. CineManiac remembers both for each video.

Can I change how subtitles look?

Yes: Settings → Subtitles sets the size, colour, outline or box, and how high they sit, separately for portrait and landscape.

Does it keep playing when I leave the app?

Yes. Going home starts Picture in Picture, and locking your device keeps the sound playing with controls on the lock screen. Both can be turned off in Settings.

A video won't play or shows corrupted pictures.

Try turning off Settings → Hardware Decoding and opening it again. If it still fails, email us with the details above.

Where is my watch history kept?

Only on your device. CineManiac has no account and no servers. Settings → Clear Playback History removes it.
