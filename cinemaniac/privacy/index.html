Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

CineManiac collects no data. Your videos, subtitles and playback history stay on your iPhone or iPad. All playback happens on the device. CineManiac has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never see, receive or sell anything about you.

Who we are

CineManiac is made by JKHoodles Pty Ltd, Australia ("we"). This policy explains what CineManiac does with information on your device. If anything here is unclear, contact us at andy.kim.jkhoodles@gmail.com.

What CineManiac does not collect

CineManiac does not collect, store on any server, transmit or share personal data. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not use your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts or location. That is why its App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

Your videos and subtitle files

  • Files you choose. CineManiac only reads the files and folders you give it: videos you open from the Files app or another app, folders you add (for example from iCloud Drive, a USB drive or a network share), and subtitle files you add. They are read on your device to play them and are never uploaded.
  • iCloud Drive. If you play a video that is stored only in iCloud, iOS downloads it to your device first; that happens between your device and Apple.
  • Shared with CineManiac. A video you send to CineManiac with AirDrop or the Share menu is copied into CineManiac's own folder, where you can rename, move or delete it.

What is stored on your device

  • Playback history for each video: where you stopped, your audio and subtitle choices, subtitle timing, picture shape and flip, and when you last played it, so CineManiac can continue where you left off. Settings → Clear Playback History removes all of it.
  • To recognise a video after you move or rename it: its file number, size and a short fingerprint calculated from the start of the file. These never leave your device.
  • Permission bookmarks for the folders and subtitle files you added, so CineManiac can open them again.
  • Thumbnails and the length of each video, so lists show quickly.
  • Your settings.

None of this leaves your device through CineManiac. Like any app's data, it may be included in backups you make to iCloud or a computer, under Apple's terms. Deleting CineManiac deletes all of it, and any videos in CineManiac's own folder.

Purchase check

CineManiac is a paid app sold through the App Store. When it starts, it asks Apple, through StoreKit on your device, to confirm that your copy came from the App Store. We never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Features provided by iOS

The title of the video playing appears on your lock screen and in Control Center through iOS. Picture in Picture, AirPlay and screen mirroring are provided by iOS, and the video goes only to the device you choose. None of these send anything to us.

Children

CineManiac collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything CineManiac keeps is on your device and under your control.

Changes

If CineManiac ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
