Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

OneShot collects no data. Your photos, videos, projects and exports stay on your iPhone. OneShot has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never see, receive or sell anything about you.

Who we are

OneShot is made by JKHoodles Pty Ltd, Australia ("we"). This policy explains what OneShot does with information on your device. If anything here is unclear, contact us at andykkt@gmail.com.

What OneShot does not collect

OneShot does not collect, store on any server, transmit or share personal data. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. That is why its App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected".

Your photo library

  • Reading. With your permission, OneShot reads your photo library so you can choose photos and videos for a project. If a chosen item is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may download it to your iPhone; that happens between your device and Apple. The media is only used on your iPhone to build your video.
  • Saving. When you export, OneShot asks for permission to add the finished video to your library. It only adds; it does not read anything back.

You can change either permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Files and sounds

Audio you import from the Files app is copied into OneShot's own storage on your iPhone so your projects can keep using it. You can delete it from the Files tab of the sound picker. Built-in sound effects ship inside the app.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your projects and their edits.
  • Imported audio, as above.
  • Small preferences: your recent and saved sound effects, and whether OneShot has already asked you for a rating in this version.
  • A count of finished free exports, kept in the iPhone's keychain so the free allowance is not reset by reinstalling.

None of this leaves your iPhone through OneShot. Like any app's data, it may be included in backups of your iPhone that you make to iCloud or a computer, under Apple's terms. Deleting OneShot deletes your projects and imported audio.

Purchases

OneShot Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages subscriptions; we never receive your payment details, name or Apple Account. OneShot only asks Apple, through StoreKit on your device, whether Pro is active. Apple's handling of purchases is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and ratings

When you tap Share, iOS shows its share sheet and the video goes only where you send it. The rating prompt is Apple's own; any rating or review you leave goes to Apple.

Children

OneShot collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live).

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Everything OneShot keeps is on your iPhone and under your control.

Changes

If OneShot ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version of the app is released, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.
